AI Game Translator

Translate English and Turkish using your camera, microphone and images

AI Game Translator brings together camera, microphone and image translation tools to help you understand games and videos.

CAMERA
Point your phone at subtitles, select the text area and view a translation. Listen to translated text with Turkish text-to-speech.

LISTEN
Recognize English speech through your phone microphone, view a Turkish translation and hear it aloud. The default free Listen engine processes speech on your device. Recognition and translation models need to be downloaded before first use.

ADVANCED TRANSLATION
Try the optional on-device English → Turkish engine. It requires a separate 1.83 GB model download, a 64-bit runtime and sufficient available memory/storage. A memory check is applied for devices in the approximately 6 GB RAM class and above. Results vary by device and dialogue; the fast engine remains available when advanced inference is unavailable or late.

IMAGES AND HISTORY
Translate text in a selected image. Review, share or clear recent translations.

PERSONALIZATION
Light and dark themes, Turkish and English interface options, text size and speech speed controls. Available voices depend on your phone's TTS engine and installed languages.

TRIAL AND PRO
Try 3 minutes of camera translation, a separate 3 minutes of Listen and 10 image translations. These limits are measured inside the app; the trial does not automatically start a subscription. Monthly, yearly and lifetime Pro purchase options are available. Subscriptions renew until canceled. Google Play displays current local prices and terms before payment.

IMPORTANT DETAILS
The app uses your phone camera and microphone; it does not directly capture audio from a console. Accuracy depends on audio quality, accents, music, effects and context. Instant, perfect or exhaustive slang translation is not guaranteed. Expressive voices and automatic speaker identity are not guaranteed. Optional cloud features require a separately configured service and internet; provider charges may apply. This is not an official product of any game or console manufacturer.
